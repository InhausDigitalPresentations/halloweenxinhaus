Stage 01 · Thu 15 — Mon 19 Oct

Whisper.

"If the last horror film you saw wasn't scary enough…
wait until the lights go down."

We announce a Halloween screening with no title. For five days, every post hides one clue: a year, a sound, a shadow on the stairs, a reflection. Fans start guessing, everyone else starts wondering, and we never confirm a thing.

  • 01The TapeAn audio-only Reel that opens on "Headphones on." A cassette clicks, then hiss, breathing, a creak on the stairs. It's built to send the guesses everywhere except the right film.
  • 02Wrong NumberWe re-cut our Scream phone-call video with a new conversation full of false leads. Fans will name every horror film but this one.
  • 03It MovedPosters that look static but move so slowly that people scroll back to check. A head turns by degrees; a figure lifts off the floor.
  • 04Mid-ScreamStatics of terrified audiences with popcorn in the air. The only clue is a tiny reflection in someone's glasses. Zoom in.

Stage 02 · Tue 20 — Wed 28 Oct

Play.

"If watching wasn't scary enough…
try playing before the lights go down."

Don't Lose Your Head.

A web game with free tickets as the prize. Guide your cursor or thumb through a corridor against the clock, without touching the walls. Each level is narrower, with less time, and stranger than the last. Touch a wall and a jump scare takes over the screen. Then we tell you how many people were scared before you, and invite you to enter the draw, or just book.

Stage 03 · Sun 25 — Thu 29 Oct

Dare.

"If the spider walk wasn't scary enough…
You haven't seen it like this before."

The motto, at full strength. Each poster names an iconic horror moment, and fans will connect the dots. Then it promises that this time will be worse. The posters run one a day, animated, with paid retargeting to everyone who played the game. This is where curiosity turns into bookings.

CTA · Last seats. Book the mystery screening.

Stage 04 · Fri 30 Oct — Sun 1 Nov

Scream.

"If the film wasn't scary enough…
look who's sitting next to you."

The promise pays off. The lights go down, the title appears, and someone takes the empty seat. Our job these three nights is to catch every reaction, and use nights one and two to sell night three.

  • 01Night-vision camsLow-light cameras on the audience catch the moment guests notice who's sitting beside them. Ticket T&Cs and signage cover filming.
  • 02The reveal momentWe film the room as the title card lands: the gasps, the "no way", the friend who instantly wants to leave.
  • 03Influencers & mediaSeated where it'll happen to them. They film the arrival and the exit; phones stay away during the film.
  • 04"One word."Quick vox pops on the way out: One word for that. Who did you grab?
  • 05Spoiler-free StoriesLive from the lobby with no title and no plot, just faces. "Night one survivors: 0 comment."
  • 06Dark Room comboBlack popcorn in a sealed box: Do not open until the lights go down. Subject to F&B.
CTA · Final night. Book now.
Hands off to Reveal: three nights of footage.
Stage 05 · Mon 2 — Sun 8 Nov

Reveal.

"Still not scary enough?
Wait until next year."

Now we can say it. We confirm the film, reward everyone who guessed right, and turn the footage into the campaign's biggest moment. Then we roll the motto forward into a waitlist.

  • 01The RevealThe poster series returns with the answer written underneath, and one line for the people who guessed: "You were right. Were you ready?"
  • 02The supercutA night-vision montage of the moment each guest noticed who was sitting next to them. This is the hero Reel.
  • 03Who grabbed who?A UGC prompt: tag the friend who screamed loudest. We reshare the best confessions.
  • 04The Scare CountThe final tally of heads lost in the game and screams on the night. A data story with a wink.
  • 05Same time next year?A waitlist for the next mystery screening. Three nights become a ritual.
